### Dhanasar Prong 1: Substantial Merit and National Importance
Under Matter of Dhanasar, 26 I&N Dec. 884 (AAO 2016), the Petitioner must show that the proposed endeavor has both substantial merit and national importance. The endeavor is defined by the Petitioner and must be specific enough for USCIS to assess its importance.

The Petitioner describes the endeavor as applying artificial intelligence, cloud architectures and biomedical big data to create state-of-the-art disease risk prediction tools, drawing on his experience with national health data on millions of patients, including rheumatoid arthritis, type 2 diabetes and chronic kidney disease. (p. 1) He names planned topics of artificial intelligence analysis of medical records, brain wave analysis and gene sequence function. (p. 1) He also describes his work as serving various companies and collaborating with hospitals to provide medical analytics and risk assessment. (p. 1)

Substantial merit: likely met. Early-stage disease risk modeling in medicine has evident merit in health and in the economy.

National importance: not met on the present record. The endeavor is stated broadly, as several topics and as work for a company, rather than as one defined project. The Petitioner argues that the endeavor would reduce US healthcare costs through prevention and early treatment. (p. 1) That is argument only, and no economic or other evidence tied to his work is described. The file contains general material, namely an article on hepatitis B, a Centers for Disease Control and Prevention article on eliminating hepatitis B, a commentary on the impact of COVID-19 on the United States, industry reports and references to ARPA-H. (p. 1) None of it is described as tied to the Petitioner's own work or as showing national or global implications of his specific endeavor. Under Dhanasar, general importance of a field does not establish the national importance of the particular endeavor.

Because prong 1 is not met, the petition cannot succeed as presented, and prongs 2 and 3 are not reached for purposes of approval. They are nonetheless addressed below so that the firm may prepare the record. [Attorney: decide how to define the endeavor narrowly and what evidence of national or global implications to obtain, such as expert opinions that address the specific endeavor and economic or policy evidence connected to it.]

The self-description of serving various companies and collaborating with hospitals is self-serving and uncorroborated. (p. 1)

### Dhanasar Prong 3: Balance Favoring a Waiver of the Job Offer and Labor Certification
