### 1. Qualifying relationship: spouse of a US citizen, legally valid marriage

### 2. Marriage still subsisting: the docket conflict
Status: unresolved; the file contains conflicting descriptions.

A USCIS reading of the 2009 docket described the case as a divorce filing 'Disposed of in Error' (p. 1). The 2009 verified petition states that the parties 'are not divorced nor is there pending any legal separation or dissolution proceeding' (p. 1). The note describes the 2009 case as one for custody, child support and medical insurance, marked 'disposed' in 2010 (p. 1). The 2010 agreed judgment is described as dealing only with the child's financial care (p. 1).

These descriptions cannot be reconciled on the present record. The 2009 petition and the 2010 judgment, as described, point to a subsisting marriage, but neither is proof that no dissolution decree exists (p. 1). The docket description raises the possibility that a dissolution was entered, filed or later vacated (p. 1). The note also does not say who at USCIS made the reading or when (p. 1). If the marriage ended by divorce, the self-petition would depend on timing rules for divorce and on a connection between the divorce and the abuse, so the point is critical. [Attorney: obtain certified copies of the full chronological case summary and every order from the Indiana court, and a certification of no record of divorce, before filing. Also obtain the source of the 'USCIS reading'.]
