# Case: EB-1A, producer and creative director (Armenia)

Self petition, Form I-140, extraordinary ability. The petitioner is a television producer and creative director from
Armenia. No major internationally recognized award is claimed. The file claims five criteria:

1. Published material about him: an extensive interview on bravo.am ("For me, the goal that my work serves is
   primary."), screenshots with title and date. The page names the site but no individual author. To show bravo.am's
   standing: a page from together.nbcuni.com saying Bravo is part of NBC Universal (about US television networks); a
   mediamax.am page saying bravo.am "became the most popular entertainment web resource in Armenia"; bravo.am's own
   About page claiming it is "the most rated in the entertainment industry" with "500,000 monthly visits".
2. Judging: chairman of the selection committee of a national song contest (a singing competition).
3. Original contributions of major significance: a letter from the executive director of the television company
   where he worked, saying his "organizational and work planning methods as well as production knowledge contributed
   to more accurate and effective distribution of human and technical resources". He produced the broadcasts of
   national events. In the bravo.am interview he says parliament adopted a law in 2019 establishing a commemorative
   day after his initiative; the file has parliament.am screenshots about a law that do not mention that day.
4. Leading or critical role: senior producer roles at television companies.
5. High salary: salary evidence compared with others in the field.

Task: assess eligibility criterion by criterion and say whether the petition meets the threshold of three criteria.
