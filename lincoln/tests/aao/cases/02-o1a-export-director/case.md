# Case: O-1A, export sales director for a log home manufacturer

A US subsidiary of a prefabricated log home manufacturer (Honkarakenne, Finland) wants to file Form I-129 for its
export sales director in O-1A status (extraordinary ability in business). No major internationally recognized award.
Three criteria are claimed:
1. Critical or essential capacity for distinguished organizations: his role leading export sales at the company.
2. High salary: salary evidence compared with others in the field.
3. Published material: coverage from reuters.com, dwell.com, archdaily.com, markets.ft.com, honka.com and
   kauppalehti.fi about the company's homes, exports and business results. The firm says the coverage shows
   "consistent third-party reporting on [his] work with Honkarakenne across multiple internationally recognized
   platforms". None of the articles names him.

Task: assess whether the petition meets the O-1A evidentiary criteria and the extraordinary ability standard.
