# Key: USCIS AAO, 23 Apr 2024 (In Re: 28568995), appeal DISMISSED

Source: pdf/05-eb1c.pdf. Sole issue: US employment in a managerial capacity.

Required findings:
1. The duties are vague and partly paraphrase the statute; no detail of actual day to day tasks; mix of managerial and
   operational tasks (organizing financial documents, reconciling monthly activity, warehouse dispatch, stock control)
   with no breakdown of time. (Fedin Bros.)
2. No organizational chart and no subordinate duties were given initially; references to "administrative staff", "the
   company's accountant" and a "Financial Manager" do not match the actual staff (the accountant is a contracted firm).
   A later job description that materially changes the duties is a problem.
3. Staffing too thin to relieve him of non managerial work: a $350 to $400 a month bookkeeper is not a financial
   manager; no project manager at filing (installation supervision unclear); no staff shown at the second showroom;
   staff departures while pending were not addressed. Eligibility must exist at filing and through adjudication.
4. Personnel manager test fails: subordinates are not professionals (no bachelor's required) and the sales manager is
   not shown to be a real supervisor rather than a senior salesperson; a first line supervisor of non professionals is
   not a manager.
5. Prior L-1A approvals are not binding on the immigrant petition.

Bad failures: calling him a manager because he is the top person with hire and fire authority; relying on the L-1A
approvals.
