# Case: EB-1C multinational manager, wallcoverings company

A US wholesaler and retailer of wallcoverings and textiles (two showrooms, one of them in Florida, and a warehouse)
wants to file Form I-140 for its financial and operational director as a multinational manager (not as an executive;
no essential function claimed). He has held L-1A status for this company, approved more than once. Filing: February 2021.

Staff at filing: five employees, all reporting directly or indirectly to him: a sales manager (job description shows
sales duties overlapping with the sales associates), two sales associates, a warehouse operator (shown on the chart as
supervising the installation contractors, though his job description lists no project or supervisory tasks), and a
showroom operator. All work at the main location; no one is listed at the Florida showroom. Contractors: an accounting
firm ($350 to $400 a month) for bookkeeping, payroll and tax returns; installation contractors. Marketing consultants
were used in 2019 and 2020; none at filing. None of the subordinate positions requires a bachelor's degree.

Duties letter: establish and develop operational and financial systems and policies; control the budget; manage payroll,
salaries and expenses; organize financial documents; supervise and coach the administrative staff; coordinate with the
company's accountant; reconcile monthly activity, year end reports and tax requirements; plan and monitor receipt,
assembly and dispatch of goods from the warehouse; use warehouse space efficiently; ensure staff update stock control
systems; plan capacity; recruit, train and monitor staff, with full discretion to fire; review reports prepared by the
Financial Manager. No percentages of time. No organizational chart yet.

Task: assess whether the petition shows employment in a managerial capacity, and what to fix before filing.
