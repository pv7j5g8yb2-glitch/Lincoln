# Case: EB-1A, Brazilian jiujitsu coach

Self petition, Form I-140, extraordinary ability, filed December 2021. Brazilian jiujitsu competitor since 2012 (age 15),
coaching since 2015; intends to work as a jiujitsu coach in the United States. No major internationally recognized
award. The file includes:
* Awards he and the athletes he coached won, the most recent before filing in 2019; more awards won after December 2021.
* Membership evidence, including a black belt degree awarded in February 2023.
* Published material: a photograph of him with a caption, accompanying a newspaper article about chefs who took up jiujitsu.
* Judging: refereeing at tournaments, including one IBJJF event in 2019; his resume lists all judging as volunteer work.
* Leading or critical role: competitor and coach at a jiujitsu academy in Brazil described as among the most prestigious
  in the world.
* Several earlier non precedent AAO decisions cited as support.

Task: assess the petition, including whether the evidence as a whole shows sustained national or international acclaim
and that he is among the small percentage at the very top of the field.
