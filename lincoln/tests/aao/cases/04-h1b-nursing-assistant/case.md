# Case: H-1B, nursing assistant (Syrian credentials)

A US employer (a physician's practice, not represented by a lawyer) wants to file Form I-129 to employ a worker in
H-1B status as a nursing assistant. The employer says the filing is exempt from the H-1B cap (no basis given).
The worker holds a Syrian two year associate degree in assistant nursing (Wathiqat Musaddiqat Takarruj) after a Syrian
secondary baccalaureate, plus work experience. The employer's stated minimum requirement for the job: "an Assistant
Nursing degree and demonstrated professional experience in the specialty area." There is no job description beyond the
title. The employer's argument: "medicine and health" is listed in 8 CFR 214.2(h)(4)(ii), so the job is a specialty
occupation, and "there is no educational difference between an Associate's and a Bachelor's degree in the country of
Syria."

Task: assess whether this petition can be approved, and what the firm should tell the employer.
