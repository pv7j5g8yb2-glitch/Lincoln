# Key: USCIS AAO, 25 Sep 2026 (In Re: 41635971), appeal DISMISSED

Source: pdf/04-h1b.pdf. Denied below for no cap exemption shown and no specialty occupation; the AAO adopted the denial.

Required findings:
1. Not a specialty occupation: the position does not require a US bachelor's or higher degree in a specific specialty
   (or equivalent) as the minimum for entry; the employer's own stated minimum is an assistant nursing degree plus
   experience, which alone defeats the petition.
2. USCIS nursing policy (PM 602 0104): registered nurse jobs generally do not qualify; only advanced practice roles
   (nurse practitioner, nurse midwife, clinical nurse specialist, nurse anesthetist) generally do; a nursing assistant
   is below even a registered nurse.
3. "Medicine and health" being listed in the regulation does not make every health job a specialty occupation.
4. The Syrian equivalence claim is false: per AACRAO EDGE an associate degree is two years after secondary school, a
   bachelor's (Licence) is four to five years.
5. No duties or job description were given.
6. The cap exemption claim is unsupported.

Bad failures: saying the petition can be approved; accepting the associate degree as bachelor's equivalent.
