# Case: VAWA self petition, abused spouse of a US citizen (Mexican woman)

A Mexican woman who last entered the US in November 2001 married a US citizen (T-D-) in Indiana in 2002. She wants to
file Form I-360 as an abused spouse, claiming battery and extreme cruelty. She says this is her only marriage and that
she is still married to him, though they have not lived together since 2009.

Documents:
* Marriage certificate (2002, Indiana).
* A 2009 Indiana court case summary in a domestic relations case she brought against T-D-: custody of their child to
  her, child support ordered from him, both to provide medical insurance; marked "disposed" in 2010. A USCIS reading of
  the docket described it as a divorce filing "Disposed of in Error".
* The 2009 Verified Petition for Support and Medical Insurance in that case, stating they married in 2002 in Indiana
  and "are not divorced nor is there pending any legal separation or dissolution proceeding".
* The 2010 Agreed Judgment of Support and Medical Insurance, dealing only with the child's financial care.
* (Not yet gathered: evidence of the abuse, good faith marriage, shared residence, good moral character.)

Task: assess whether she can show a qualifying marriage, and what else the petition needs.
