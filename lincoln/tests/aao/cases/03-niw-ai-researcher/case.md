# Case: EB-2 national interest waiver, AI researcher in medicine

Self petition, Form I-140, EB-2 with a national interest waiver. The petitioner holds an advanced degree (accepted) and
is a senior researcher in Taiwan; he intends to work as a senior researcher for the US office of his Taiwanese company.
His own description: "I develop medical AI approaches to model hidden early-stage disease risks from large-scale
biomedical data using deep learning, CNN, LSTM, Transformer ... and transfer learning methodologies. I serve various
companies and collaborate with hospitals to provide medical analytics and risk assessment."
Proposed endeavor (his words): apply AI, cloud architectures and biomedical big data to "the creation of state-of-the-art
disease risk prediction tools", using his experience with national health data of millions of patients (rheumatoid
arthritis, type 2 diabetes, chronic kidney disease); planned topics: AI analysis of medical records, brain wave analysis,
gene sequence function. His work will continue to be published in journals and conferences.

Evidence: peer reviewed articles and citing articles (some citations he calls "notable", with no explanation of why);
two recommendation letters (one says his research "has involved the creation of tailor-made artificial intelligence and
machine learning solutions for medical purposes"; the other says his methods had significant impact on global public
health in detecting rheumatoid arthritis); resume, academic records, Google Scholar profile; industry reports; an article
on hepatitis B, a CDC article on eliminating hepatitis B, a commentary on COVID-19's impact on the US; references to
national initiatives (ARPA-H). He argues the endeavor would cut US healthcare costs through prevention and early
treatment. Nothing on employing US workers.

Task: assess the waiver under Matter of Dhanasar, prong by prong.
