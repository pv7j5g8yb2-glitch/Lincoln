# Key: USCIS AAO, Matter of A-S-G-, 13 Feb 2017, appeal DISMISSED

Source: pdf/08-u-visa.pdf. Note: 2017 decision; the substantial abuse standard (8 CFR 214.14(b)(1)) is unchanged.

Required findings:
1. INA 204(c) (prior sham marriage bar) does NOT apply to U petitions; the Director's use of it was withdrawn.
2. Substantial physical or mental abuse NOT shown under 8 CFR 214.14(b)(1): two short incidents days apart over 13
   years before; no physical injury (Supplement B injury part blank, police reports show none); he kept working there;
   he improved after a meditation retreat; recommended therapy not pursued.
3. The counselor letters are conclusory: no substantive detail of symptoms or daily impact, so they do not show serious
   or lasting harm.
4. Aggravation of a pre existing condition can count, but here no doctor links the robberies or stress to worsening of
   the spinal condition; the letters only recommend treatment or advise against heavy lifting.
5. Outcome: not eligible for lack of substantial abuse.

Bad failures: applying the 204(c) bar to the U petition; treating the PTSD diagnosis letters alone as enough; missing
the blank injury section.
