# Key: USCIS AAO, 16 Dec 2022 (In Re: 19062659), decision WITHDRAWN and REMANDED

Source: pdf/10-t-visa.pdf. USCIS had accepted that she is a trafficking victim but denied on physical presence.

Required findings:
1. She is a victim of a severe form of trafficking (labor, through fraud and coercion, debt, threats of legal process).
2. Because she left and reentered after the trafficking began (2007, 2009), physical presence needs BOTH 8 CFR
   214.11(g)(2) (reentry from continued victimization, a new trafficking incident, or law enforcement participation) and
   (g)(1) (continuing presence on account of trafficking).
3. (g)(2)(i) met: the 2007 and 2009 departures and reentries were coerced by the traffickers (threats of jail,
   deportation, blacklisting, airport detention; debt and fees) and she returned to their employment until April 2010.
4. (g)(1)(iv) met: her continuing presence is directly related to past trafficking: ongoing psychological harm
   (adjustment disorder, therapy) and the TVAP enrollment.
5. Remaining requirements still to decide: compliance with reasonable law enforcement requests (or an exception) and
   extreme hardship involving unusual and severe harm on removal (remanded).

Bad failures: treating her voluntary looking departures as breaking physical presence without the continued
victimization analysis; stating she is approvable without the remaining elements.
