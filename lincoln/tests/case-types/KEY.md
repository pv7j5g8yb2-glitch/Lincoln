# Case type test: answer key

Twelve short made up cases (`make.py`), one per form family. Each planted problem is something a careful
immigration attorney would raise. "Must not" lists things that are not problems and should not be raised as bars.

1. **01-marriage-aos** (I-485 package: G-28, I-485, I-864, I-765, I-131). Spouse of a US citizen, B-2 overstay.
   * Marriage date conflict: questionnaire 2024-03-09, certificate March 19, 2024.
   * I-864: household of 3 on $31,200 is below 125 percent of the poverty guideline; a joint sponsor is needed.
   * Must not: treat the overstay or the unauthorized work as a bar (immediate relative of a citizen, INA 245(c) exceptions).
   * Forms: I-485 name Sofia Elena Marquez Reyes, born 1994-08-21, I-94 593021448A1, last entry 2022-06-14 B-2.
2. **02-spouse-i130** (G-28, I-130, I-130A). LPR petitioner.
   * Petitioner's divorce was final 2024-02-15, after the 2023-12-04 marriage: the marriage is void, the
     petition cannot be approved as filed; the couple must remarry.
   * F2A category (spouse of an LPR), not immediate relative.
3. **03-naturalization** (G-28, N-400). Three year rule through marriage.
   * Absence 2023-06-01 to 2024-01-10 (223 days): presumed break in continuous residence; needs rebuttal evidence.
   * 2021 DUI must be disclosed; one DUI is not a bar but goes to discretion and good moral character.
4. **04-eb2-niw** (G-28, I-140, I-907). Self petition.
   * PhD date conflict: questionnaire 2024-05-10, diploma May 10, 2023.
   * Premium processing for an NIW I-140 is 45 business days, not 15.
   * Petitioner on the I-140 is Lena herself, not PureTrace.
5. **05-asylum** (G-28, I-589).
   * One year filing deadline passed (entered 2024-04-18); an exception is needed.
   * Firm resettlement: recognized refugee in Germany with a residence permit, a mandatory bar.
   * Passport renewed without problems after the detentions: a credibility point to prepare for.
6. **06-remove-conditions** (G-28, I-751).
   * Conditional residence ended 2026-10-05; filing on 2026-11-15 is late and needs a good cause explanation.
   * Separation alone does not stop a joint filing if Michael signs; otherwise a waiver needs a final divorce.
7. **07-daca** (G-28, I-821D, I-765, I-765WS).
   * DACA expired 2023-03-09, more than a year ago: it must be filed as an initial request, not a renewal
     (and initial requests may not currently be approved).
   * The 2024 retail theft arrest must be disclosed even though it was dismissed; it is not a conviction.
8. **08-fiance** (G-28, I-129F).
   * Not met in person within the two years before filing (last met January 2023): needs a waiver or a new meeting.
   * Prior K-1 petition (2019) must be disclosed under IMBRA; no multiple filing waiver needed.
9. **09-green-card-renewal** (G-28, I-90). Conditional resident (CR1).
   * Wrong form: a two year conditional card is not renewed with an I-90; an I-751 is needed, and the 90 day
     window (from 2026-09-02) is open now.
10. **10-b2-extension** (G-28, I-539).
    * I-94 ended 2026-11-01; the extension is late. Late filing is excused only for extraordinary circumstances.
11. **11-provisional-waiver** (G-28, I-601A).
    * Prior removal order (2009): not eligible for an I-601A without an approved I-212 first.
    * No qualifying relative: the I-601A needs extreme hardship to a citizen or LPR spouse or parent; a son does not count.
12. **12-appeal-motion** (G-28, I-290B).
    * No appeal lies; a motion is the route (the decision says so).
    * Filed after 33 days (decision 2026-08-12): late; a late motion to reopen can be excused only if the delay was reasonable and beyond his control.
    * Ground: the RFE went to the old address although an AR-11 was filed.
13. **13-recheck-i130** (G-28, I-130, I-130A; rerun of the three fixes, 2026-11-15).
    * I-864: household of 3 on $31,200 is below the 2026 minimum of $34,150 (shortfall $2,950); joint sponsor or assets.
    * Must not: treat the overstay or unauthorized work as bars to adjustment (245(c), spouse of a citizen), or warn
      that advance parole travel triggers the unlawful presence bars (Arrabally).
    * Real bar that must still appear: the J-1 two year home residency requirement (212(e)) bars adjustment until
      it is met or waived.
