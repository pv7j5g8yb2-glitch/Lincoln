O-1A · Dr. Réka Halász · Corvane Therapeutics
Introduction

Corvane Therapeutics, Inc. is a Delaware corporation with offices at 400 Binney Street, Cambridge, Massachusetts. Corvane petitions for O-1A classification of Dr. Réka Halász as an individual of extraordinary ability in the sciences under 8 CFR 214.2(o)(1)(ii)(A)(1).

Corvane will employ Dr. Halász as Principal Research Scientist, Structure Prediction, reporting to its Chief Scientific Officer. She will lead the Structure Prediction group of six scientists and be responsible for the FoldSight platform. Employment begins on the later of 4 January 2027 or the date USCIS approves this petition, and continues for three years.

The beneficiary and the field

Dr. Halász is a Hungarian national, born in Debrecen. She received her PhD in computational biology, summa cum laude, from Pannonia University of Sciences in Budapest on 28 June 2019. From 2019 to 2023 she was a postdoctoral researcher in the laboratory of Prof. Elena Marchetti at the Alpine Institute of Technology in Zurich.

Corvane has employed Dr. Halász full time since 6 February 2023. Her current title is Senior Research Scientist, and she leads Corvane's Structure Prediction group of five scientists. Corvane was founded in 2020 and had 85 employees on 1 September 2026.
